## ✨ 최종 디자인 프롬프트 템플릿 세트 (The Chaos to Control 기반)

이 템플릿은 모든 이미지 생성 작업에 적용될 수 있도록 **구조적 규칙(VCVM)**과 **스타일 지침('Chaos to Control')**을 명확하게 분리하여 설계되었습니다.

### 🖼️ 프롬프트 구조 개요 (Template Structure Overview)

모든 최종 프롬프트는 다음의 4가지 필수 섹션으로 구성되어야 합니다:
1.  **[CONCEPT & MOOD]**: 핵심 감정 및 시각적 목표 정의 (Chaos to Control 적용)
2.  **[VISUAL SYSTEM]**: 브랜드 시스템 규칙 적용 (VCVM 기반)
3.  **[COMPOSITION & LAYOUT]**: 구체적인 레이아웃 및 요소 배치 지시
4.  **[STYLE MODIFIERS]**: 최종 렌더링 품질 및 스타일 지정

### 🚀 템플릿 A: 핵심 컨셉 프롬프트 (Core Concept Prompt)

이 템플릿은 영상의 **핵심 감정(Focus/Escape)**과 **주요 구성 요소**를 정의하는 데 중점을 둡니다.

```prompt
[CONCEPT & MOOD]: Cinematic, high-contrast visual representation of 'Chaos to Control'. The image must visually embody the tension between overwhelming disorder and focused, intentional control. Mood: Intense, intellectual, dynamic, yet structured. Focus on the transition from chaotic energy (background/noise) to clear, sharp focus (foreground/control element).

[VISUAL SYSTEM]: Utilize the Cortexa Visual Consistency Model (VCVM). Primary Color Palette must strictly adhere to #2C3E50 (Dark Slate) and accent with a controlled use of high-contrast white/light gray for structure. Typography should feel technical and authoritative.

[COMPOSITION & LAYOUT]: Implement a strict split layout: Left side represents the 'Chaos' (dynamic, blurred, chaotic elements), Right side represents the 'Control' (sharp, organized, geometric structure). Use diagonal lines or sharp visual breaks to emphasize the transition zone. The main focal point must be the controlled element on the right. Aspect Ratio: 16:9.

[STYLE MODIFIERS]: Ultra-detailed photorealistic render. High dynamic range (HDR) lighting with deep shadows and precise highlights. Sharp focus on foreground elements. Cinematic depth of field. Style reference: Neo-futuristic data visualization.
```

### 🖼️ 템플릿 B: 상세 레이아웃 프롬프트 (Detailed Layout Prompt)

이 템플릿은 특정 레이아웃과 구체적인 시각적 요소를 지정하여 **VisualConsistencyScore**를 높이는 데 중점을 둡니다.

```prompt
[CONCEPT & MOOD]: Focused control emerging from complexity. The scene should feel like a highly organized system being analyzed. Mood: Analytical, clean, high-tech precision.

[VISUAL SYSTEM]: Strict adherence to VCVM rules. Use #2C3E50 as the dominant background/system color. All structural lines and interfaces must be rendered in crisp white or light gray. Avoid excessive color noise.

[COMPOSITION & LAYOUT]: Split screen composition (60% Chaos Left, 40% Control Right).
- Left Side (Chaos): Abstract, flowing lines, blurred digital noise, overlapping chaotic shapes using dark tones. High visual energy.
- Right Side (Control): Clean geometric grid, sharp vector lines, organized data panels, clear focal point. Minimalist and precise.
- Transition: A distinct, sharp vertical or diagonal line separating the two zones where the focus shifts from chaos to control.

[STYLE MODIFIERS]: Hyper-detailed UI/UX design aesthetic. Sharp edges, high fidelity rendering. Use subtle glow effects on control elements. Lighting should be directional, emphasizing depth and structure. Style reference: Technical schematic visualization.
```

### 🖼️ 템플릿 C: 타이포그래피 강조 프롬프트 (Typography Emphasis Prompt)

이 템플릿은 썸네일의 핵심 메시지를 전달하는 **타이포그래피**와 **정보 구조**를 강조할 때 사용합니다.

```prompt
[CONCEPT & MOOD]: High-impact data visualization for a system breakdown/control narrative. Mood: Urgent, informative, authoritative.

[VISUAL SYSTEM]: Primary text elements must use a clean, technical sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat or similar). Color emphasis: Use #2C3E50 for main titles and high-contrast white for critical data points. Ensure all text is sharply legible against the background chaos.

[COMPOSITION & LAYOUT]: Title/Key Message should occupy the right side (Control zone). Use large, bold, geometric typography to convey authority. The left side should contain abstract visual representations of 'Chaos' acting as context or texture behind the main message. Clear hierarchy is essential: Chaos provides context; Control delivers the solution.

[STYLE MODIFIERS]: Minimalist yet dense information display. Sharp typography. Digital interface aesthetic. High contrast ratio. Focus on legible text structure over pure artistic rendering.
```
