## 🖼️ 이미지 생성 프롬프트 템플릿 세트 (The Chaos to Control Framework)

아래는 **시스템 흐름(Chaos)**과 **결과 데이터(Control)**의 대비를 극대화하는 데 초점을 맞춘 3가지 핵심 프레임워크입니다. 모든 프롬프트에 기본 스타일 가이드라인을 포함하여 일관성을 유지해야 합니다.

### 1. 메인 레이아웃 프레임워크 (The Dichotomy Layout)

이 프레임워크는 좌우 분할 구조를 가장 명확하게 지시하며, 시스템적이고 전문적인 느낌을 강조합니다.

**[Template Name: System Flow Visualizer]**
```prompt
A professional, high-contrast visual representation based on the 'Chaos to Control' framework. The image must be strictly divided into two vertical zones: a left zone representing chaotic process flow and a right zone representing controlled data output. Use a dominant color palette of Dark Slate (#2C3E50) for structure and deep shadows.
**Left Zone (Chaos/Flow):** Dynamic, abstract lines, swirling energy, complex network diagrams, blurred motion, visual representation of uncontrolled variables or raw input. Use vibrant, energetic accents against the dark background.
**Right Zone (Control/Data):** Clean, structured, organized data visualization, clear geometric shapes, precise metrics, organized flowcharts, and stable system outputs. Use a lighter, cooler palette for contrast, emphasizing clarity and order.
Style: Digital Art, System Design Blueprint, High Contrast Minimalism. Aspect Ratio: 16:9.
```

### 2. 프로세스 강조 프레임워크 (The Flow & Control Emphasis)

이 프레임워크는 시스템의 '흐름'과 '통제'라는 핵심 메시지를 시각적으로 직관적으로 연결하는 데 중점을 둡니다.

**[Template Name: Process Transformation]**
```prompt
Visual metaphor illustrating the transformation from chaos to control. The scene should depict a dynamic transition. On the left side, show chaotic elements (e.g., tangled wires, abstract noise, scattered data points) rapidly converging. This area should be visually turbulent and energetic, colored in deep slate tones. On the right side, these elements are channeled into a highly organized, clean pipeline or matrix of structured data flowing smoothly. The transition point must be sharp and dramatic.
Color Palette: Primary use of Dark Slate (#2C3E50) for process lines and highlights. Use subtle gradients to show energy transfer.
Style: Cinematic Flow Diagram, Abstract Motion Graphics, Data Visualization Aesthetic. High detail, sharp focus.
```

### 3. 데이터 시각화 프레임워크 (The Control Focus)

이 프레임워크는 결과물(데이터/통제)의 명확성과 전문성을 극대화하여, 시스템 안정성 및 KPI 측정을 강조합니다.

**[Template Name: Control Data Matrix]**
```prompt
A highly organized, sophisticated data matrix visualization that represents system control and stability. The composition should be dominated by geometric shapes, clean lines, and structured grids. The visual focus is on the precise measurement of results (Control) derived from complex inputs (Chaos). Include subtle overlays or background elements hinting at underlying complexity, but the overall feeling must be one of absolute order and mastery.
Color Palette: Predominantly Dark Slate (#2C3E50) as the base, with bright, crisp accent colors (e.g., electric cyan or stark white) used exclusively for data points, metrics, and flow indicators.
Style: Futuristic UI/UX Design, Isometric System View, Minimalist Data Visualization. High resolution, sharp edges.
```
